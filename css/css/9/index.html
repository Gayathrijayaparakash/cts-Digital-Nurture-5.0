June Neighborhood Updates

The city council has announced a month of neighborhood events focused on wellness, arts, and public service. Residents can attend outdoor classes, join cleanup drives, and meet local organizations at the community resource fair.

The library will host reading circles for children and adults throughout the month. The parks team will also open new shaded seating near the playground and add extra recycling stations during weekend events.

Volunteers are invited to help guide visitors, distribute event maps, and support accessibility desks. Training sessions will be held every Thursday evening at City Hall.
