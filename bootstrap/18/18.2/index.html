Collapsible Accordion

This accordion uses Bootstrap's accordion component and JavaScript-powered collapse behavior.

accordion accordion-item data-bs-toggle="collapse"

An accordion stacks sections vertically and lets users expand one section at a time.

The accordion uses Bootstrap's collapse plugin through data-bs-toggle="collapse".

data-bs-parent keeps the behavior accordion-like by closing the open panel when another panel opens.
